Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how ("SmallClick", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website or contact us. If you are in Mexico, please read the Spanish version (Aviso de Privacidad), which applies to you.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, type of business, the package you're interested in, your language and currency, and anything you write to us.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited and approximate location, through cookies or analytics tools if we use them.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information, and our website is not directed to children under 13.

How we use it

  • To send the coupon or quote you requested and reply to your inquiry.
  • To provide, deliver and support the services you buy.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To send text messages about your request, only if you checked the SMS consent box. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • To keep our website secure and improve it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, under their own privacy and security terms, such as: Google (email and spreadsheets), Stripe (payments), our website hosting provider, and email or messaging tools. We may also disclose information when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for up to 24 months after our last contact, and client records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you marketing messages. Depending on your state (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Texas and others), you may have additional rights, such as knowing what we collect, deleting it, correcting it, and opting out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. Send your request to . We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

International users

We serve clients in the United States and Mexico. Your information may be processed in either country and by providers located in the United States.

Changes

We may update this policy. We'll post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date.

Transferencias y remisiones

No vendemos tus datos personales. Para operar compartimos (remitimos) tus datos con proveedores que los tratan por nuestra cuenta, como Google (correo y hojas de cálculo), Stripe (pagos), nuestro proveedor de hosting y herramientas de correo o mensajería, algunos ubicados en los Estados Unidos. Derechos ARCO y revocación del consentimiento

Tienes derecho a Acceder a tus datos, Rectificarlos, Cancelarlos u Oponerte a su tratamiento (derechos ARCO), así como a revocar tu consentimiento y limitar el uso o la divulgación de tus datos. Para ejercerlos, envía una solicitud a con:

  • Tu nombre y un medio para responderte.
  • Un documento que acredite tu identidad o, en su caso, la de tu representante.
  • Una descripción clara del derecho que deseas ejercer y de los datos involucrados.

Te responderemos dentro de los plazos que establece la ley. Si consideras que tu derecho no fue atendido, puedes acudir a la Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, autoridad en materia de protección de datos personales.
